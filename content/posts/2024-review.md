hello humans. 2024 is very very soon coming to an end so I've decided to write a short review of all the cool new things that happened to me since I've got some free time to kill before the new year.

## joining college

soooooo, I managed to get into a decent enough college. a few months before actually coming to the campus, I decided to join communities of my college to find and interact with my potential friends.

it was probably the best decision I made. I managed to interact and then eventually be friends with a lot of cool people. so it was probably one of the nicest things that happened this year.

## re-discovering city-pop/80s nippon music

in 2020, I first discovered city pop music through my beloved anime [city hunter](https://myanimelist.net/anime/1470/City_Hunter). back then I didn't explore much of this genre. mostly listened to the same songs on repeat. soon enough I got fed up with it and moved on.

now fast forward to this year, one of the mixes of [xerf xpec](https://www.youtube.com/@TheXMusicArchive) came into my youtube recommendations. I don't exactly remember which video was it but it was a really good mix. I then went on to explore their channel and found about jazz fusion genre and a lot of popular artists like [takanaka](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCh2rvH6MGKYC-UHjTA1-eIQ), [casiopea](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCVc0F74TcYIT_g7n0_G_tmQ), [t-square](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC_zauy6iNEZ0twT8o_FubnQ) and many others :D

my current favourite nippon artists are:

## git gud at computers

got access to laptop 2 years ago and since then, I have given my 100% to explore cool new technologies and I feel like I have learned and improved a lot at it.

learned a lot of new libraries, frameworks, tools. now I'm confident enough to build anything that comes to mind. just need enough motivation to keep building it ;-;

## be more disciplined

I kind of failed on this one as I was too late to this but I feel like I have made a significant improvement in my self.

swapped the time I spent doom scrolling on youtube or lurking in a random chatroom to partially reading books, reading about random stuff I come across and dip deeper into it and controlling my screen time.

I regained overall self-control. I didn't know it was *this* easy to let go of addiction. you *just* need to stop; tell your brain to stop and you're good to go. no fancy tool can help you in any way as long as you do not make up your mind to stop. it's not that hard.

wish I had realized this a bit earlier. my life would have become so much better and easier.

this was probably the best year in last decade(?) of my life :)
